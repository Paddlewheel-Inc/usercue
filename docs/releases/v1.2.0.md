## 🎉 UserCue 1.2.0 is here

You asked for a better way to sort through the pile, tell people when their idea ships, and hear from more places than one board. This is that update, and it's our __biggest one yet__.

Here's everything new. 👇

## New

### 🧹 Sorting the pile

- 🔍 **Fewer duplicates, before they happen.** As someone writes a request, we show similar ones right there. People vote on what already exists instead of starting a new thread.

- 🔀 **Merge the ones that slip through.** Votes and comments move over to the post you keep, and the old link points people to the right place.

- 🎯 **A way to decide what's next.** Score each request by __impact and effort__, sort by priority, and leave notes only your team can see. Don't work that way? Turn it off in Settings, or keep it on just the boards that need it.

- 🏷️ **Find things fast.** Filter by tag, assignee, or minimum votes, then save the view you keep coming back to.

- 🗳️ **Votes that mean something.** Set how many open requests one person can back at once, so votes show what people care about most.

### 💌 Closing the loop

- 🚀 **Tell people when it ships.** Everyone who backed a request gets a heads up when it lands. Shipped posts get a badge, and your changelog entries link straight to the requests they close.

- 📬 **A weekly summary.** Your team gets a digest of new requests, top votes, and what shipped. Each person can opt out from their account page.

- ✍️ **Write your changelog the easy way.** Switch to __Markdown__ and write the whole update in one go. Start a section with New, Changed, Fixed, or Removed as a heading, or put one in square brackets at the start of a line, and it turns into a tag.

### 🌍 Feedback from everywhere

- 📧 **Feedback by email.** Customers write to an address on your domain and it lands on your board as a request. It runs through Resend.

- 🧩 **Feedback on your own site.** Add a floating widget, or drop your board, roadmap, or changelog right into a page.

- 📦 **Bring your history with you.** Import from Canny, Featurebase, Productboard, or any CSV or JSON file. Votes, authors, tags, and dates come along when your file has them.

### 🔌 Plug into your stack

- 🔔 **Chat and automation.** Post to Slack, send __signed webhooks__ to Zapier, Make, or your own systems, and check the audit log whenever you need to know who changed what.

- 🤖 **AI that stays out of the way.** Totally optional. Add any OpenAI-compatible key and get thread summaries, tag suggestions, and a first draft of your changelog from the requests you're closing.

- 📚 **Docs for developers.** The public API, webhook signatures, and Zapier and Make recipes are all written up in the API guide.

## Changed

- ⚙️ **Settings finally make sense.** We sorted everything into clearer tabs. Notifications has email, the digest, and your chat tools. Integrations has webhooks, email in, AI, embeds, and import. Team has staff invites.

- ✉️ **Email does more.** What you set up under Notifications now powers invites, the weekly digest, and shipped alerts, not just invites.
